<objective>
Point the real-browser net at everything Phase 16 claims: flip checkboxes against a real server and
a scratch project, watch the pending markers and the bar appear, read the config file on disk to
confirm what the copy admits, apply for real and watch the poll — not the POST — clear the bar,
collect a 409 against a held slot, revert, force a write failure, and race the poll — writing down
exactly what happened, row by row, with the full suite green.

until a browser proves it, and the two claims that matter most in this phase — that the panel never
announces convergence the server has not confirmed, and that a checkbox never bounces — are pure
timing behaviors no source pin can reach.

Output: a per-row recording in 16-06-SUMMARY.md, the eight manual rows ticked in 16-VALIDATION.md
with dates, and a green full suite.
</objective>
